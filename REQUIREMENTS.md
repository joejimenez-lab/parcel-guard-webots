## Proposal audit corrections

The audit found three behavior gaps: delivery was recorded before locking, the owner temporary token could be reused, and an open-door reset could extend the bolt too early. Those paths are corrected and covered by policy and Webots regression cases. The report companion and final slides also used an older role allocation; they now follow the written proposal.

The six-week plan and weekly integration meetings are planning commitments, not software features. The final handoff follows the proposed modeling, integration, and evaluation stages. No retrospective meeting attendance or individual work history is claimed. The class presentation, Q&A, and upload remain activities for the team.
